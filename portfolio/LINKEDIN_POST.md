I built **MTA TransitPulse**, a browser dashboard and PostgreSQL case study on NYC subway reliability and passenger impact from 2022–2025.

The analysis found that passenger-weighted weekday journey-time performance rose from **83.88% to 85.40%**. Even so, the 2025 MTA customer estimate implies **3.24 billion signed net additional passenger minutes** across the measured weekday trips.

To turn those figures into an investigation plan, I combined reliability, passenger-time burden, and peak service delivery in a documented 2025 score. **F, B, and Q** ranked first under the proposed weights and stayed near the top across five scenarios. **D** warranted a separate reliability review.

The project includes four MTA datasets, a validated Python/SQL pipeline, sensitivity testing, a public four-view browser dashboard, and a Power BI project. The rankings are a starting point for diagnosis, not a claim that a specific incident caused the delays or that an intervention would recover a stated number of minutes.
